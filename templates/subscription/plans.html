What you are actually buying

{# Careful: the plan cards above DO gate two things (bulk email campaigns, advanced analytics - see PLAN_CATALOG in api/subscription.py), so this line must not claim every feature is on every plan. Neither is listed below. #}

The numbers on the cards are the limits. This is the work you do inside them.

Quotes and contracts

Emailed as a private link. Your client accepts a quote or signs a contract in the browser, with no account and no password.

Invoices that get paid

An accepted quote raises the invoice itself. Clients pay by card, MTN, Airtel or M-Pesa through PesaPal, or wire your bank.

Time on tasks

Start and stop a timer on a task, or add the hours afterwards. They roll up per task, per project and per person.

Client portal

Invite a client and they get a login that shows their projects, their tasks and the work waiting on their approval.

Deliverables report

What was delivered, what is late and who did it, for one project or a whole client. Prints to PDF, exports to .xlsx.

Email that sends itself

Quotes, invoices, contracts, task assignments and comments each email the person who needs to know.
