Deactivate account

Deactivate ?

  • They will not be able to sign in, and any session they currently have open ends on their next click.
  • They are hidden from active lists such as their team's member list, and they stop using a paid seat.
  • Their invoices, comments and time entries are untouched, and old records still show their real name.
  • This is reversible - you can reactivate the account at any time.

Permanently delete account

Permanently delete ?

This cannot be undone. It only works for an account with no history at all - if they have any activity, comments, time entries, invoices or a team membership, the delete is refused and nothing changes.

In almost every case Deactivate is what you want instead: it keeps the account and its records, hides it from active lists, stops it signing in, and can be undone.
