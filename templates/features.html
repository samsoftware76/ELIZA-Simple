01

Win the work

Quotes and contracts, both answered from a link in an email.

Quotes

  • Line items with a description, a quantity and a unit price, in the currency you pick per quote.
  • A validity date, a quote number and your own business name and logo on the document.
  • Emailed as a private link. The client reads it in a browser with no account and no password.
  • Accept, or decline with a written reason that comes back to you by email.
  • Print or save the quote as a PDF from the same page.

Contracts

  • Signed in the browser: the client types their full name, ticks a consent box and draws a signature.
  • ELIZA records the typed name, the signature image, the timestamp and the signer's IP address.
  • Once a contract is sent, its text can no longer be edited - what they read is what they sign.
  • Amend a sent, signed or declined contract and ELIZA issues it as a new numbered version.
  • Void an unsigned contract to kill its link. A signed one cannot be voided or deleted.

02

Do the work

Projects, tasks and the hours that go against them.

Projects

  • Belong to a client, carry a status and a start and end date.
  • Progress is the share of their tasks that are done, not a number you type.
  • Assign team members to a project with a role on it.
  • Mark complete, and reopen if the client comes back.

Tasks

  • Priority, due date, assignee and a description.
  • Move through To Do, In Progress, Review and Complete.
  • Comments on the task, with an email to the people involved.
  • Anything past its due date is counted as overdue on the dashboard.

Time

  • Start and stop a timer on a task; one running entry per person per task.
  • Or add hours after the fact with a start time, an end time and a note.
  • Hours roll up per task, per project and per person.
  • Your hours this month sit on the dashboard next to overdue tasks.

03

Get paid

An invoice the client can settle on the page they are already looking at.

Invoices

  • Raised by hand, or automatically from the quote the client just accepted.
  • Due dates follow the payment terms you set once on your profile.
  • Sent as a private link, printable to PDF, and markable as paid by hand when someone pays you off-platform.
  • Each invoice keeps its own currency; ELIZA never adds two currencies together.

How they pay

  • Card, MTN, Airtel and M‑Pesa mobile money through PesaPal, from a button on the invoice.
  • Or a bank transfer: your bank name, account name, account number and SWIFT code print on the invoice.
  • A paid invoice emails you straight away with the amount and the method.
  • The Wallet page totals what you have collected, what is still outstanding and what is overdue.
  • ELIZA never holds your money. It goes to your PesaPal merchant account or your own bank.

04

Keep clients informed

So the "any update?" email stops arriving.

Client portal

  • Invite a client by email and they get their own login, showing only their projects.
  • They see each project's tasks and how far along it is.
  • They approve work you have put up for review, and comment on a task.
  • Revoke a client's access from their client record whenever you want.

Reports

  • Deliverables: what was delivered, what is late and who did it, for one project or a whole client.
  • That one prints to PDF and exports to Excel as an .xlsx workbook.
  • Project status, task distribution, time tracking, project timeline and overdue tasks, four of them with a CSV export.

05

When you are not working alone

Your team

  • Invite people by email; the invite link expires after seven days.
  • Give each one your own job title instead of a fixed role name.
  • They see the account's clients and projects, not their own empty copy of the app.
  • Remove a member or revoke an invite that has not been used.

Client email

  • Write one message and send it to the clients you tick.
  • Every copy carries that client's own unsubscribe link, and an unsubscribed client drops off the recipient list.
  • Quotes, invoices, contracts, task assignments and comments all send their own emails.
