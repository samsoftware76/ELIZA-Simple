The quote becomes the invoice

The client reads the line items and clicks Accept. ELIZA copies those same lines into an invoice, marks it sent, and puts it on their next screen to pay. You get an email naming the invoice it raised.

{# Contract.signer_name / signature_image / signer_ip / signed_at in models/billing.py; contract_edit() in api/contracts.py refuses to edit a sent contract. #}

Contracts signed in the browser

They type their full name, tick the consent box and draw a signature. ELIZA keeps the name, the signature image, the timestamp and the IP address on the contract, and refuses to edit the text once it is sent.

{# PesaPal button + bank block in templates/portal/invoice.html; the custody note matches templates/wallet.html. #}

Card, mobile money or your bank

PesaPal handles cards and MTN, Airtel and M‑Pesa. Your bank name, account number and SWIFT code print on the invoice for clients who wire. ELIZA never holds the money - it goes to your PesaPal merchant account or your bank.
