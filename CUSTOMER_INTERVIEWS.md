# ELIZA Customer Discovery Script — Stage 2 (samsoft-check)

**Target:** 10–15 freelancers / small agency owners in Uganda (or East Africa
broadly) who invoice clients and get paid for services.

**Rule of the whole conversation: do not pitch ELIZA.** You are not selling
anything today. You are collecting true stories about their past. If they ask
"is this for an app you're building," it's fine to say "yes, eventually — but
right now I just want to understand how you actually do this," and redirect.

**Kill/proceed rule:** if fewer than ~2 in 3 people volunteer a specific, real,
recent painful memory (not a general complaint) *unprompted*, the problem isn't
sharp enough yet — go back and rethink Stage 0, don't start building.

Time budget: ~15–20 minutes each. Record with permission, or take notes live.

---

## 1. Warm-up — get them talking about their real business (2 min)

- "Tell me a bit about what you do and who you usually work for."
- "Roughly how many clients are you working with right now?"

(Just building rapport and confirming they're really in-segment — don't rush.)

## 2. Their actual current process (5–7 min) — the core of the interview

- "Walk me through the last time you sent a client a quote or price estimate.
  What did you actually do, step by step?"
- "And the last time you invoiced someone for finished work — same thing, walk
  me through it exactly."
- "What are you using to do that right now? Can you show me?" (Excel? WhatsApp?
  Paper? A notebook? Nothing formal?)
- "How do you currently keep track of who's paid you and who still owes you?"

*(Listen for the workaround — the messier and more manual it is, the sharper
the opportunity. Don't suggest better ways yet.)*

## 3. The late/non-payment moment (4–5 min) — probing the sharpest known pain

- "Tell me about the last time a client paid you late, or you had to chase
  someone for money. What happened?"
- "What did you actually do about it? Did it work?"
- "How did that make you feel, dealing with it?" *(Let them say "awkward,"
  "stressful," etc. in their own words — don't feed it to them.)*
- "Has a client ever disputed an invoice or claimed they never got it? What
  happened?"

## 4. Money movement specifically (3–4 min) — probing the Bonsai/PesaPal angle

- "When a client pays you, how does the money actually reach you — mobile
  money, bank transfer, cash, something else?"
- "Has a payment ever taken longer to reach you than you expected? Tell me
  about that."
- "Have you ever lost money to fees you didn't expect, on a payment coming in?"

## 5. The compliance/tax angle (2–3 min) — Uganda-specific hypothesis

- "Do you deal with URA / tax registration for the income from this work?"
- "Have you ever needed an invoice to look a certain way, or needed a TIN on
  it, for tax or compliance reasons? Tell me about that."
- "Has that ever caused a problem — with a client, or with your own records?"

## 6. Branding / trust with their own clients (2 min) — HoneyBook-complaint angle

- "When you send something official to a client — a quote, an invoice — does
  it look like it's from *your* business? Or does it look like it's from
  whatever tool you used to make it?"
- "Would that matter to you? Has a client ever commented on it?"

## 7. What they've already tried (2 min) — JTBD workaround signal

- "Have you ever tried a tool or app for this — quotes, invoicing, client
  tracking? What happened? Why did you stop, or why are you still using it?"
- "What would it have to do, for you to actually switch away from what you use
  now?" *(A story about a real past switch is gold; a hypothetical answer here
  is weak — note which you got.)*

## 8. Close (1 min)

- "Is there anything about getting paid, or managing clients, that's annoying
  or costly that we haven't talked about?"
- "Would it be OK if I followed up with you in a few weeks as I learn more?"
  *(This is your pilot-user list if the evidence says to proceed.)*

---

## After each interview — log it immediately

Fill one row per person in the evidence ledger (see `/samsoft-check` skill):

| # | Segment fit | Specific real pain described? (Y/N + quote) | Current workaround | Money-movement pain? | Tax/TIN pain? | Branding matter to them? | Tried another tool? | Would follow up? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

After all interviews: count how many gave a real, specific, recent story vs. a
vague or hypothetical answer. That ratio is your go/no-go signal — not your gut,
not mine.
